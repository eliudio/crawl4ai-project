This exciting self-guided audio tour invites you to lace up your trainers and embark on a 10K journey across the iconic bridges of the River Thames. Kicking off on Friday, March 8, 2024, and available until December 31, 2026, participants will have the opportunity to explore the stunning sights of London at their own pace.  \n  \nAs you traverse a scenic route from Tower Bridge to Vauxhall, you\'ll cross 11 picturesque bridges, including London Bridge and Waterloo Bridge, while soaking in panoramic views of major landmarks like the Tower of London and St Paul\'s Cathedral. With GPS-triggered audio commentary, you\'ll dive into the rich history of the Thames and its surrounding attractions, making every step both fun and educational. Whether you\'re a seasoned runner or just looking for a unique way to stay active, this event promises an exhilarating and enriching experience. Don\'t miss your chance to take on this challenge and earn an optional finisher medal! Sign up today and discover the beauty of London like never before. \nRead more\n![Three women are jogging on a modern footbridge in bright daylight with smiles on their faces.
